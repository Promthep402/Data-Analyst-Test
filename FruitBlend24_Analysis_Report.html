Calculation logic

Raw item names are normalized to the 5 SKU dimension values. Platform and promotion are decoded from the rate code. Net revenue = gross revenue less platform commission. Variable cost = weekly fruit cost + packaging + labor. Gross profit = net revenue - variable cost - fixed monthly kitchen overhead.

Forecast is the trailing 3-month average as a transparent baseline. Inventory uses a 5% service buffer, with a separate 20% waste-reduction target.

Operating plan

Buy: set weekly par levels by kitchen and SKU from the trailing 8 weeks, with a 5% demand buffer. Replenish high-velocity Watermelon and Pineapple more frequently; use smaller batches for Mixed Berry.

Control: review waste daily; reduce next-day orders when waste exceeds 3% of cups handled. Discount or pause slow inventory near cut-off.

Protect profit: require every promotion to show positive incremental contribution before rollout.

Recommended decisions

Pricing

Run controlled +THB 5 Mixed Berry and +THB 3 Watermelon tests.

Portfolio

Keep the five core SKUs, but separately track the 374 non-core raw rows and prune weak RC101 bundles.

Inventory

Use kitchen-SKU weekly pars, a 5% service buffer, and a 20% waste-reduction target.

Tools and approach: Python, pandas, openpyxl, deterministic transformations, and a self-contained HTML report. Further work: causal promotion lift, weather/daypart seasonality, and row-grain validation with delivery platforms.
